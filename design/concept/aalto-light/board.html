The concept

Aalto Light

Designed for the person lying down.

In 1933 a Finnish architect built a sanatorium for patients who spent their days in bed. He coloured the ceilings because that is where they looked, kept lamps out of their eyes, bent birch so a chair would help them breathe, and turned the rooms toward the sun. Afferent is played in exactly that position: on your back, phone above you, two minutes after waking.

So the whole product borrows that idea. The world is drawn with one material and one gesture: the aalto, Finnish for wave, a free curve like a lake shoreline, built from bent laminated birch. The game starts in kaamos, the blue polar night, and every finished task bends one more layer of light into the land until it reaches kevät, spring. Your mornings stack up the same way, layer on layer, like plywood: strong because it is laminated, not because any single day was.

Hero key art, morning session at dawn (high). The bell lantern, the shoreline wave, rays as lamination lines.

Principles
  1. Design for the horizontal bodyContent lives in the upper two thirds where the eyes rest; the thumb works the lower third. Light comes from the side of the frame, never straight into the eyes.
  2. One curve per screenThe free wave is the signature. It appears once: a shoreline, a sheet edge, a streak band. Everything else is calm M3 geometry.
  3. One sun per screenPaimio yellow marks the single thing that matters now: the lantern, the Begin button, today's layer. Never two.
  4. Layers, not scoresProgress is lamination: mornings add layers, a missed day only rests the wood. No counters that break, no red, no fail.
  5. Material honestyBirch, plaster, linoleum, low daylight. Matte, soft, nothing glossy or neon. The game removes colour; play gives it back.

Illustration

STYLE prompt (reusable, plan.json "style")
Calm 2D illustration ... in the spirit of Finnish functionalist design of the 1930s: free-form wave curves, bent laminated birch plywood and the sunlit healing rooms of a sanatorium ... Every landform, shoreline, cloud, path and snowdrift is one long, smooth, asymmetric free-form curve ... Shapes are large, flat and matte like smooth painted plaster and linoleum, each with one very soft airbrushed light gradient; wooden and light-catching bands show fine parallel lamination lines ... Light is low northern daylight through tall windows ... Palette: kaamos blue #1E3150, deep fjord #0E1A2E, frost #9DB9CC, birch white #F2EEE6, birch ply #E2C79C, Paimio yellow #F2C230, spring green #9DBE8C, ceiling green #C8D6BE, dawn apricot #F3A57A, lilac haze #B4A6CE ... No outlines, no text, no faces, no red.
Do
Long single-gesture curves. Rays, aurora and steam as fine parallel lines. Big empty sky. The figure small.
Avoid
Puffy smoke, zigzag fells, glossy 3D, real furniture copied. Sprites need the v3 neutral-light suffix.
