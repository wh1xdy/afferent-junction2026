Bedtime instrument

Material 3 structure tuned like Pixel Clock Bedtime mode and Digital Wellbeing: very dark, low glare, big soft numerals and controls, one lantern accent, almost no chrome. Dynamic colour is seeded from the in-game sky instead of the wallpaper; the lantern is always the primary.

Dynamic colour: sky seeds the neutrals, the lantern is primary

Pipeline (Material colour utilities, unchanged): render the current sky frame, QuantizerCelebi (128) on the top 60% of pixels, Score picks the seed, neutral and neutral-variant palettes take its hue at chroma 6 to 15, secondary at 12 to 24, primary is always the lantern hue (#F2B544, chroma 44). Bedtime tones replace M3 dark defaults: surface 3, containers 1 / 5 / 7 / 10 / 14, on-surface 84, on-surface-variant 68. Clinic uses surface 4, on-surface 93. Colour changes ease over 4 s. Never red: state colours are harmonised to the lantern and always carry an icon and a label.
