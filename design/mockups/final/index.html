Lantern Material

The Afferent GUI system: Material 3 close to 1:1, coloured by the in-game sky, lit by the lantern.

Dynamic colour, seeded by the sky

Material Color Utilities builds every scheme. Neutral and secondary palettes come from the quantized game sky, primary is always the lantern, tertiary is the aurora. Dark schemes use bedtime tones (surface tone 3, on-surface 84). No red role exists: error is amber-orange.
